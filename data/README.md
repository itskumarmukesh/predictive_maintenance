# Data source and provenance
Dataset: CMAPSS Jet Engine Simulated Data (NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence)
Landing page: https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data
Download: https://data.nasa.gov/docs/legacy/CMAPSSData.zip
Date downloaded: 2026-10-24
License: none specified on the NASA portal (publicly accessible). Cite the paper below.
Citation: A. Saxena, K. Goebel, D. Simon, N. Eklund, "Damage Propagation
Modeling for Aircraft Engine Run-to-Failure Simulation", PHM08, Denver CO, 2008.

Files: train_FD00x.txt, test_FD00x.txt, RUL_FD00x.txt for x = 1..4, plus NASA's readme.txt and the paper PDF.

Columns (space-separated, no header): 1 unit, 2 cycle, 3-5 operational settings,
6-26 sensor measurements (21 sensors; to be verified by counting in Phase 1).
